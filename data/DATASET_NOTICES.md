# Dataset Notices

## VidTIMIT

The `data/raw/vidtimit` directory is reserved for the publicly accessible VidTIMIT source. Distribution and research usage are governed by the conditions published by the dataset maintainer at https://conradsanderson.id.au/vidtimit/. Any resulting publication must cite Sanderson and Lovell, *Multi-Region Probabilistic Histograms for Robust and Scalable Identity Inference* (LNCS 5558, 2009).

## FakeAVCeleb and LipSyncTIMIT

Do not place, mirror, redistribute, or commit either dataset to this repository. Both complete datasets require acceptance of a maintainer-controlled request process. Local paths for datasets obtained under those approvals must be supplied through configuration or environment variables and remain excluded from Git.
